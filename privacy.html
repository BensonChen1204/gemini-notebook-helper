Privacy Policy

Gemini Notebook Helper — Effective date: November 23, 2026

Overview

Gemini Notebook Helper is a personal-use application developed and used solely by its developer, Benson Chen, with his own personal Google account. This policy explains what data the application accesses, how that data is stored, and how access can be revoked. The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

The application accesses data only through Google's official Gemini Notebook API, using the single OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/notebook. Through this scope it can view and manage notebooks and their sources (web pages, YouTube videos, text, and Markdown) in the developer's own Google account. The application performs actions — such as creating a notebook or adding a source — only when instructed to do so by the developer. It does not access Gmail, Drive, or any other Google service data.

Data storage

  • OAuth credentials (the OAuth client configuration and refresh token) are stored only on the developer's own computer, in a permission-locked local store. They are never transmitted to any server operated by the application.
  • Notebook content created through the application remains in the developer's own Google account, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • No server-side collection: the application does not operate a server that collects, logs, or stores personal data.

Sharing, sale, and tracking

The application does not share personal data with third parties, does not sell personal data, and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Revoking access

Access granted to this application can be revoked at any time by visiting the Google Account permissions page, selecting "Gemini Notebook Helper", and removing its access. Revoking access immediately stops the application from accessing the account's notebooks. The locally stored credentials can also be deleted from the developer's computer at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
